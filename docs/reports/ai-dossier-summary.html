Conclusion

The AI Dossier is a practical map of AI's present — 86 proof points that AI can transform core business functions across every major industry, governed by Trustworthy AI™ principles.

What the dossier demonstrates

  • 86 concrete, industry-specific use cases
  • Real operational friction addressed — not abstract capabilities
  • Agentic shift: 32 multi-agent orchestration cases
  • Trust embedded in every risk section

What organizations should take away

  • Start with the business problem, not the model
  • Treat agentic AI as an operating model
  • Invest in foundations before scale
  • Use the catalog as inspiration, not a checklist

The strategic divide

Organizations treating AI as a point solution capture efficiency gains. Organizations treating AI as infrastructure for decision-making — with agents, governance, and work redesign — capture differentiation. The dossier is a snapshot of what AI can do today; more impressive use cases are coming.

Bottom line

The question for leaders is not whether these patterns apply, but which use cases to prioritize, what foundations to build first, and how to deploy with transparency and accountability. The competitive edge goes to those who combine technical capability with trustworthy deployment.
