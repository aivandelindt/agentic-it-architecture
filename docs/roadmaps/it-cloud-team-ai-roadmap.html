Executive summary

Agentic AI is the operating logic of tomorrow's cloud platform — not Copilot licenses without production workflows. This roadmap climbs the autonomy ladder while evolving six pillars concurrently: strategy, governance, data, platform, workforce, and change management.

Humans shift from operators to orchestrators. Every pilot declares its production path, integration pattern (overlay → by-design), and Return-on-Autonomy (RoA) metrics before funding.

Conclusion & next steps

Treat autonomy as a staged journey, not a plug-in feature. Each phase builds data fabric, talent, and guardrails for the next leap. Quick overlay wins (Assist/Suggest) build trust in months 1–4; durable value requires guardian agents, MCP agent-mesh, by-design change flows, and role redesign across all six pillars.
